# AI Work · Ritika Jindal

I'm Ritika Jindal, a Senior Product Manager in Bengaluru with ~9.5 years across engineering (Samsung R&D) and product, and an MBA from IIT Delhi.
At Gainsight I build AI products for customer success teams, including Copilot, MCP, Agent Studio, the AI Account Briefer and the Slack AI agent.

This repo holds hands-on AI projects I've built to explore how agents, retrieval and LLM-powered tools work in practice.

## Projects

### [CSM Meeting Prep Agent](csm-meeting-prep-agent/)
An agent that reads a CSM's calendar, spots upcoming customer calls, and researches each account across Gainsight, Slack and Gmail.
It writes a one-page HTML prep brief with talking points, risks and opportunities, where every claim links back to its source. [See a sample brief →](https://jindal.github.io/AI-work/csm-meeting-prep-agent/sample_brief.html)

### [Resume ↔ JD Matcher](resume-jd-matcher/)
A web app that scores how well a resume fits a job description, with a scorecard of strengths, gaps and a clear recommendation.
It then suggests edits you can accept or skip and gives back a tailored resume in the original PDF's format. [Open the app →](https://jindal.github.io/AI-work/resume-jd-matcher/)
